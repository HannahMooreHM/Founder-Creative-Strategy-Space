# Competitor and pricing landscape: productised founder strategy

Researched 24 July 2026. All prices as published on the date checked, in the currency shown.

**Sourcing note (method caveat):** this research environment's egress policy blocked direct fetching of vendor pages (only GitHub and package registries are reachable), so prices were captured via search-engine retrieval of the vendor's own pages where possible, and via third-party reviews/aggregators where not. Each row is labelled: **[V]** = captured from the vendor's own page/help centre, **[R]** = reported by third-party reviews consistent across multiple sources, **[UNVERIFIED]** = single-source or could not be corroborated. Re-check the linked pages before publishing any number externally.

## 1. Decisive summary — the price corridor for issue #14

The market splits into four clean bands, with a conspicuous gap in the middle:

| Band | What buyers get | Price corridor |
|---|---|---|
| Pure-AI DIY (no human) | Logo/brand kit, generated strategy docs | **$20–$175 one-off, or $10–$50/mo** |
| AI strategy tools (deeper output, still no human) | ICPs, GTM strategy, market research | **$99–$250/mo** (credit-metered) |
| Human-reviewed productised strategy | BrandScript/messaging engagement, strategy workshop, small strategist project | **£350 (2-hr session) → $1,500–$10,000 project; UK freelance projects £1,500–£8,000** |
| Fixed-price human sprint (agency-grade, productised) | 2–4 week brand/positioning sprint, senior-led | **£10,000 / $10,000–$15,000 fixed** |

**The gap:** almost nothing productised sits between ~$50/mo AI-only output and ~$1,500 human engagements. The obvious wedge for the three layers issue #14 must price:

- **First reading + snapshot:** the AI-only band tops out around $49–$99; a snapshot *with a human eye on it* can credibly sit at **£150–£500 one-off** — above all AI-only tools, below every human engagement found. WTP evidence (below) says the mass of small owners cluster at ~$500 for a first branded deliverable.
- **Experiment loop:** subscription comparators price ongoing AI strategy at **$49–$249/mo**; a loop with periodic human review can anchor **£100–£300/mo** without meeting resistance from any published comparator.
- **Consultant layer:** the verified market for named-human strategy work starts at **£350 for a session** and runs **£1,500–£8,000 per project** (UK freelance) with UK strategy day rates averaging **£520/day**. Escalations quoted at £500–£1,500 per piece of work are inside the corridor, not premium to it.

Anchoring language competitors use: "a fraction of the price of an agency/copywriter" (AI tools anchor against humans) and "weeks not months, fixed price" (sprints anchor against traditional agency processes of £30k+).

## 4. Willingness-to-pay evidence

All aggregated survey statistics — treat as benchmark/anecdotal, not primary data:

- **57%** of small businesses will pay about **$500** for a logo; **~18%** up to $1,000; only **14%** pay more than $1,000. **[R]** https://cropink.com/logo-statistics
- **71%** of businesses with revenue under $500k keep a monthly branding budget of **$100–$500**. **[R]** https://blog.gaggleamp.com/branding-statistics
- Most small businesses spend **$5,000–$20,000** on a full brand identity when they do commit (2026 guides); budget constraint is the stated reason founders default to Canva-style DIY. **[R]** https://synrgycreative.com/blog/what-does-branding-cost
- StoryBrand live workshop tickets: **$1,249–$4,900** per person — evidence founders pay four figures for *framework + guided application* without bespoke consulting. **[R]** https://bigorange.marketing/storybrand-certified-guide-cost/
- No published WTP survey specifically for "AI strategy + human review" hybrids was found — this is exactly the gap the issue #14 money test should measure. **[UNVERIFIED — absence of evidence, searched 24 Jul 2026]**

Read for #14: the mass market clusters at low hundreds (one-off, ~$100–$500/mo ceiling on budgets); the *funded/serious founder* segment demonstrably pays £1.5k–£15k when a credible human is attached. Price the snapshot for the first cluster, the consultant layer for the second.

## 5. How competitors frame the human-expert upsell

Five distinct patterns observed:

1. **Human tier inside the AI product (Brandmark):** $25/$65 AI-only tiers, then $175 "Enterprise" whose headline feature is *10 original concepts by the human design team*. Human involvement is the top-tier differentiator, priced ~3–7x the AI-only tier — a useful ratio.
2. **Free AI → paid AI → certified human network (StoryBrand):** the AI product is explicitly framed as "a world-class copywriter... for a fraction of the price"; the human Guides ($1,500–$10,000+) are sold on depth, accountability and implementation, not on correcting the AI. The AI is the funnel top; certification fees mean the humans pay *them* to be the upsell.
3. **Fixed-price sprint vs the agency bogeyman (Studio Foundation, Rebel Lemon, Upspire, Proof of Work):** "weeks not months", "fixed cost, no surprises", senior named humans ("led by a Creative Partner with 25+ years") — anchored against £30k+ traditional agency engagements. Seniority of the named human is the trust signal that justifies £10k.
4. **Credit-metering that makes strategy the premium unit (M1-Project, IdeaProof):** ICPs/strategies cost many credits, social posts few — the pricing itself teaches users that strategy output is the valuable artefact. Compatible with charging more when a human touches the strategy unit.
5. **Validation as a separate purchase (Wynter):** messaging testing sold apart from messaging creation ("don't guess — test on real buyers"). Supports packaging the experiment loop as its own paid layer rather than bundling it invisibly.

## 6. Gaps and caveats

- M1-Project, Osum, IdeaProof, Brandmark and Designjoy figures are third-party-corroborated **[R]**, not captured from a rendered vendor page; re-verify before quoting publicly.
- Brendan Dell and Brand Purist are "contact us" — no public anchor.
- Wynter's per-test pricing is calculator-based in-app; the "$225 mid-level test" figure circulating in reviews is **[UNVERIFIED]** as to unit (likely per-audience-block, not per whole test).
- No direct comparator was found selling exactly the hypothesised shape (AI-generated strategy reading + human consultant escalation, subscription loop) at small-founder prices — the closest are StoryBrand.ai (AI + external human network) and Brandmark (human tier in-product). That absence is the finding.
