# CONTEXT.md — The System's Shared Language & Architecture
*Set on 2026-07-24 through a grilling session. This file is the glossary and constitution every document, prompt, and future tool in this repository must obey. Decisions and their reasons live in `DECISIONS.md`.*

---

## The system in one line

A founder workspace that turns minimal business evidence into a clear, evidence-labelled strategy, records what it assumed, and guides the founder to the next most valuable decision or experiment — delivered as a Hannah-led service first, software later.

---

## The constitution

Every part of the system obeys one loop:

```
EVIDENCE          What happened?
   ↓
INTERPRETATION    What might it mean?
   ↓
DECISION          What will we change or test?
   ↓
ACTION            Do it — which produces new evidence
   ↓
(back to EVIDENCE)
```

**Constitutional rules:**

1. **Everything queues at the same door.** Nothing enters the strategy directly — not a Reddit thread, not an AI suggestion, not a founder's hunch. Everything enters as `[HYPOTHESIS]` and earns its way up the evidence ladder through experiments.
2. **One stamped side-door.** A strategist's judgment call may change the strategy without an experiment, but it must carry a **judgment stamp**: the reasoning, a confidence level, and the future experiment that could confirm or kill it. A judgment call that never names its test is just an opinion.
3. **The Cadence.** No experiment goes 🟢 LIVE until its read-date follow-up has an owner and a calendar appointment. An experiment without a follow-up appointment isn't launched — it's abandoned in advance.
4. **The Reading Day.** Experiments are read in weekly batches: read date = the first Reading Day after the sample matures. One ritual, not scattered pings.
5. **Two labels only.** `[EVIDENCE]` and `[HYPOTHESIS]` are the system's only epistemic labels. Older tags (`unvalidated`, `[UNVERIFIED]`) are plain synonyms of `[HYPOTHESIS]`.

**Engine roles:**

- **Evidence Harvest** — one unified collection sweep (the master prompt's search cascade + the Reddit engine's buyer-question discovery, merged). Runs once per client; only the monthly citation audit re-collects. Both engines are *readers* of the harvest, not competing collectors.
- **Strategy Generator** — upstream in time: always runs first, on existing material. Immediate value, no fifty-question intake.
- **Value Prop Validation Engine** — upstream in authority, downstream in time: prescribed when the offer itself is the scariest belief; its verdict outranks everything.
- **Reddit/AI Visibility Engine** — ears at the top (intake → evidence layer), hands at the bottom (activation → `E-VIS` experiment cards with scored-by metrics and read dates). The ears judge whether the hands worked.
- **Delight Compass** — scans the ledger and quote bank (lens 1) and adjacent/other industries via the Cross-Industry Lens (lens 2). Suggestions must cite evidence; they enter the backlog as 📝 DRAFT cards and never jump the queue.

## The Cross-Industry Lens `SUTHERLAND`

The system's newness engine — how strategy avoids becoming only "retrospective cumulative learning":

1. Take a **theme** from the evidence layer — it must be evidenced (e.g. "trust", backed by quote-bank entries), prioritised by what matters most to the ICP.
2. Scan *other* industries that solve the same theme for the same human feeling (a dating app's trust problem → fintech's trust rituals).
3. Propose borrowings conversationally, teaching the founder why: *"Your ICP's evidenced theme is X. Look what brand A does in industry Y. You could try Z — explore this, or stay focused on existing experiments?"*
4. Every borrowing enters as `[HYPOTHESIS]`, citing the theme that summoned it, and can only exit as a 📝 DRAFT experiment card in the backlog.
5. Offered at natural moments only — after an experiment closes, or at the quarterly — never mid-experiment.

Named thinker: **Rory Sutherland** — psycho-logic; value lives in perception; the solution is rarely where the industry is looking.

## The six judgment gates (soft)

The system always proceeds — best default, reasons shown, assumption stamped — and at these moments it signposts, honestly and without sales theatre, that a strategist would weigh what it can't:

1. **Contradictory evidence** — two evidence strata pointing opposite ways.
2. **Positioning conflict** — e.g. the logo-semantics ruling; meaning conflicts surfaced as strategic decisions.
3. **Judgment side-door use** — every stamp is by definition a strategist moment.
4. **KILL / RETHINK verdicts** — killing a belief or challenging the strategy itself.
5. **The quarterly pivot-or-persevere meeting** — the recurring strategist venue.
6. **Vision & creative direction** — weaving tactics into one coherent story.

Posture: abundance. Working with Hannah is the upsell; the gates are signposts, never toll booths. The system never punishes a founder for giving little input — it shows the cost of uncertainty in confidence labels.

## The evidence ladder

Every noun in the system sits on one rung. Only experiments and stamped judgments move things up.

| Rung | Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Source** | Where an observation came from (a thread, a review page, an interview) |
| 1 | **Evidence item** | A verified observation with provenance: quote, number, behaviour, payment |
| 2 | **Hypothesis** | An interpretation not yet earned — everything AI-generated starts here |
| 3 | **Belief** | A hypothesis the current strategy leans on (the §6.1 table) |
| 4 | **Learning** | A belief tested and written to the ledger — the only rung that compounds |

|---|---|
| **Segment** | A reachable group sharing a buying situation — what the Value Prop Engine auditions |
| **ICP** | One specific person-in-a-moment the strategy serves — the winning segment, cast |

| **Validated value** | A thing customers already validate in reviews (formerly "proposition value", template §2) |

| **Identity claim** | Who the buyer believes the choice makes them |
| **Scariest belief** | The belief whose failure would collapse most of the strategy — tested first |

| **Prediction** | The winner committed before launch — being wrong is the valuable outcome |
| **Pass-mark** | The threshold fixed before results are visible |

| **SHIP / KILL / RETHINK** | Roll out the winner / falsify the belief / park for the quarterly — unchanged |
| **PROCEED** | Passing a phase gate (VPVE). Distinct from SHIP |
| **Learning** | A customer-level lesson (never about the copy) with a "→ Feeds:" arrow |

| **Visibility opportunity** | A hypothesis about a venue — never evidence until verified |
| **Citation audit** | The monthly record of whether AI engines name or cite the brand |
| **Evidence Harvest** | The single unified collection sweep both engines read from |
| **Strategy version** | A published snapshot of current beliefs and decisions (v1 → v1.1 → v2) |

## Glossary — craft terms

The words the method uses when doing the strategy work itself. Same rule: this table is the dictionary; the strategy template is the phrasebook — it may *simplify* these meanings in place, never contradict them.

| Term | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **Onliness** | The sentence "we're the only ___ that ___", finished with checkable facts |
| **The zag** | When the whole category turns right, you turn left — scale what only you can scale |
| **8-word test** | If the onliness won't compress to ≤8 words, it isn't an onliness yet |
| **Tomorrow Test** | Could the market leader say this line tomorrow? If yes, it's a label, not a position |
| **Trust drip** | One small checkable fact; enough drips fill the trust bucket — you never claim, you accumulate |
| **Sleeper Receipt** | The receipt the market leader *cannot* copy because their business model forbids it |
| **The remarkable thing** | The specific, slightly surprising fact customers retell at dinner |
| **Quote bank** | The store of verbatim customer quotes mined by the Evidence Harvest |
| **Evidence strata** | The six kinds of quote the bank must cover: emotional extreme · daily tax · category core failure · learned distrust · transformation sought · persistent residue |
| **Voice rules** | The customer's dialect goes in; company jargon stays out (formally: register rules) |
| **Comfort language** | Technical terms customers themselves use — precision reads as care; keep them |
| **Gate test / gate moment** | Would a customer say the line aloud at the real place the decision happens? That place must be evidenced, never imagined |
| **Outlier file** | Logged posts that massively beat their account's normal numbers — what the audience rewards |
| **Packaging first** | Hook, title and thumbnail decided before the content exists; no good cover → don't write the book |
| **Hook** | The first line/three seconds; its only job is to earn the next line — and it must pay off honestly |
| **Answer box** | The dashed box holding a question only the client can answer, with why-we-ask in grey |
| **"In one line" (Minto)** | The one-sentence conclusion under every heading; the boxes alone tell the whole strategy |
| **Borrow File** | The cross-industry borrowings section (template §6.8) — evidenced theme → other industry's solution → draft card |
| **Delight Compass** | The two-lens suggestion engine: ledger scan + Cross-Industry Lens; output only ever becomes draft cards |
| **Challenger Pass** | The pre-delivery quality gate: a rival strategist's voice hunts for what the document missed |
| **E-VIS card** | An experiment card for a visibility action (a comment, a content page) — no activation lives outside the tracker |
| **The backlog** | Where draft cards wait their turn — borrowings and suggestions enter here, never the first-3 queue |

**Retired words:** "REVISE" and "iterate" (→ TWEAK; gates once written PROCEED / REVISE / KILL now read PROCEED / TWEAK / KILL) · "proposition value" (→ validated value) · "unvalidated" and "[UNVERIFIED]" as distinct labels (→ `[HYPOTHESIS]`) · "Delight Radar" (→ Delight Compass) · "Red-Team Pass" (→ Challenger Pass).
